# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different experiences and perspectives.
- Give feedback about ideas and work without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately through the website contact form or LinkedIn profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action in response to behaviour that violates this code.
